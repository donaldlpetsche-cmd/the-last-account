# Season One Architecture

## Locked structure

- Ten novels.
- Each novel approximately 45,000–60,000 words.
- Each centers on one high-stakes operation.
- Every book stands alone and resolves its central mission.
- Every book advances the hidden Season One structure.
- Emotional and physical consequences persist.

## Season adversarial spine

AM Strategic Advisory is the hidden Season One firm. Its public business is credible, legal, and valuable. Its compartmentalized special-matters capability manufactures outcomes for powerful clients through illegal and lethal means.

Addison Moody authorizes the hidden work. The reader learns this before Jack.

## Book One state change

At the end of *The Last Account*:

- Tomas Weaver's operation fails.
- Stephen Bentley's framing and death are exposed.
- The ledger and supporting digital evidence survive.
- Rylan Adams is neutralized and implicated.
- Addison knows Jack is the leading explanation for the failure.
- Addison does not know Jack's permanent crew or method.
- Jack does not know Addison controls the organization.
- Hattie Baker reopens or creates an active file on Jack.
- Reese survives but requires surgery.
- Marcus's trust in Jack is damaged.
- The crew remains family, but the fracture is not erased by victory.

## Planned season pressures

### Addison and Jack

Attraction begins at the end of Book One or in Book Two. It is genuine, not a con performed solely for advantage. Both conceal defining truths. The reader understands the danger before Jack does.

### Hattie and Jack

Hattie accumulates evidence rather than making intuitive leaps. She tracks proximity, unavailable originals, implausible outcomes, and recurring signatures. She must not identify the permanent crew too early.

### Anton's past

A former intelligence contact or handler may become a bridge between Anton's past and Hattie's investigation around Books Three or Four. Details are provisional.

### Matthew Mallory

Matthew's reintroduction is a future emotional and operational collision. Jack recognizes him in surveillance imagery after more than thirty years. Exact book placement and mission are unresolved.
