# Mandatory Chapter Drafting and Continuity Workflow

This procedure is mandatory. A chapter is not complete and may not be presented as approved until the post-chapter continuity audit is finished.

## Before drafting

### 1. Establish starting state

Record:

- Exact or relative date and time.
- Location.
- Characters physically present.
- POV character.
- What each relevant character knows.
- Objects and evidence in each person's possession.
- Active injuries and physical limitations.
- Cover identities, credentials, vehicles, communications, and access rights.
- Relationship and trust state.
- Immediate objective.
- Constraints inherited from prior chapters.

### 2. Compare authority sources

Check:

- Series Bible.
- Character Bible.
- Approved chapter map.
- Every prior manuscript chapter.
- Master continuity ledger.
- Object/evidence chain.
- Knowledge matrix.
- Injury and relationship ledger.

### 3. Resolve contradictions before prose

Do not draft around a known contradiction. Correct the map or canon through explicit change control first.

## During drafting

- Maintain viewpoint discipline.
- Track movement through physical space.
- Record every transfer of evidence or equipment.
- Do not give a character information early.
- Do not allow an injury to disappear.
- Do not import a legacy fact because it is familiar.
- Flag new canon created by the prose.

## Mandatory post-chapter audit

### Timeline
- Does elapsed time work?
- Do travel, surveillance, research, and recovery periods fit?
- Does the chapter preserve all prior times and deadlines?

### Locations
- Can every character physically reach each location?
- Are entrances, exits, floors, rooms, shafts, vehicles, and sight lines consistent?

### Character knowledge
- How did each character learn each fact?
- Has reader knowledge leaked into character action?
- Has Jack withheld or disclosed information consistently?

### Objects and evidence
- Who possesses every key, phone, photograph, ledger, copy, credential, weapon, tool, or vehicle?
- Is provenance intact?
- Has anything duplicated, vanished, or appeared without transfer?

### Injuries
- Are pain, mobility, treatment, and recovery realistic and persistent?
- Does physical performance match current condition?

### Identities and names
- Are legal names, aliases, cover roles, organizations, and spellings consistent?
- Is Weaver, Newman & Gaines, LLC used formally and Weaver Newman used as shorthand?

### Relationships
- Does trust change for a demonstrated reason?
- Does family history influence behavior without being overexplained?

### Causality
- Backward check: does every event have a supported cause?
- Forward check: what consequences must persist?
- Antagonist check: does the opposition act on its own knowledge and incentives?

### Voice
- Does the prose read like a human commercial thriller rather than generated text?
- Remove symmetry, repetition, overexplanation, generic emotion, and manufactured cleverness.

## Acceptance rule

After the audit:

1. Repair contradictions in the chapter.
2. Update all live ledgers.
3. Record new canon.
4. Record unresolved questions.
5. Only then present the chapter or begin the next chapter.

Continuity may not be deferred until manuscript completion.
