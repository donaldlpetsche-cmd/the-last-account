# POV Voice Cards

## Purpose

Each viewpoint character has a locked interior voice. In a blind read, a reader should identify the POV within two paragraphs.

Each card specifies: what the character notices first in a room, how they measure time, native metaphors, what they never think in words, sentence tendencies, silences, and dialogue tics.

Voice cards are authoritative. When a chapter draft conflicts with the card, the card wins unless a scene-specific reason is documented in the acceptance record.

---

## Jack Mallory — Primary POV

**Registers:** dominant in C; present in A and B.
**Charm register:** C only.

- **Notices first:** exits, sight lines, who defers to whom, what a room costs and who paid, the incentive structure in play, the person nobody is watching.
- **Measures time by:** what needs to happen before the next move, and how long a person can be pressured before they change posture.
- **Native metaphors:** legal, structural, and mechanical. He thinks in leverage, standing, cause of action, load-bearing walls, torque.
- **Never in words:** the word *love*. The words *my family*. Any direct statement of fear. Any admission that Matthew's departure still moves him.
- **Sentence tendencies:** clean, measured, slightly formal. Complete sentences even under load. He does not think in fragments; he thinks in sequences.
- **Silences:** long, deliberate, and often the loudest thing on the page. Jack uses silence in dialogue as a tool.
- **Dialogue tics:** understated. Asks the smaller question first. Never raises his voice. When he softens, the crew notices.
- **In Register B:** listens more than he speaks. Cooks. Drives. Watches. Interior warms; the prose does not narrate the warming.
- **Book One arc constraint:** withholds. The POV must let the reader feel the omission without stating it. Jack's chapters should register a low, persistent tension of things unsaid.

## Nina Rodriquez

**Registers:** B and A. C only when running a persona.
**Charm register:** B (dry technical wit); C (when in character).

- **Notices first:** signal, cameras, network topography, badge patterns, someone's phone hand, the make of a laptop across the room.
- **Measures time by:** intervals, latency, refresh rates, and how long ago she last slept.
- **Native metaphors:** systems, protocols, packet loss, failure modes, redundancy.
- **Never in words:** any statement about belonging. Any admission that the crew is her only family.
- **Sentence tendencies:** technical when precise, sarcastic when tired, sharp when scared. Her sentences accelerate under stress rather than shortening.
- **Silences:** she gets quiet when truly afraid. Loud sarcasm means she is okay.
- **Dialogue tics:** technical shorthand undefined for the reader. Deflects sincere affection with a joke or a subject change. Says "yeah" more than "yes."
- **Physical tells rendered through her own POV:** pulls sleeves over her hands, avoids eye contact when someone tells her she matters, dismantles small electronics while thinking.
- **In Register B:** the crew makes her food. She lets them.

## Marcus Lowell

**Registers:** A, B, and C. Any room.
**Charm register:** B (dry paternal); C (executive gravity).

- **Notices first:** threat vectors, distances to cover, weapon presence, number of people in a room, exits, driver-side approach angles.
- **Measures time by:** rotations, watches, engagement windows, and his children's schedules.
- **Native metaphors:** military and mechanical. Cadence, formation, sector, discipline, breach, dwell time.
- **Never in words:** any admission that he cannot leave the perimeter. The word *scared* about himself. Doubt about Jack, spoken.
- **Sentence tendencies:** economy. Complete sentences. Rarely a fragment. No adverbs when a verb will do.
- **Silences:** long. Marcus's silence during a planning conversation is a decision-making instrument the crew reads.
- **Dialogue tics:** understated. Uses names deliberately. Calls Betty by her name in his own head, not "my wife."
- **In Register B:** at his own house. Grills. Corrects the children softly. Watches Jack watching Reese climb the porch rail.
- **Book One arc constraint:** command trust. His POV must register the moment he realizes Jack has been withholding, and the prose must not soften that recognition.

## Anton Rominov

**Registers:** A and B. Rare C.
**Charm register:** B (deadpan, terse warmth).

- **Notices first:** the lock, the hinges, the tolerances, the age of a mechanism, whether a room has been altered from its original plan.
- **Measures time by:** cycle counts, cure times, and how long ago he last spoke to Katya.
- **Native metaphors:** mechanical. Actuation, wear, seating, seizure, pressure.
- **Never in words:** any reference to what he did before the crew. Any statement about loyalty. Katya's name in the same thought as the CIA.
- **Sentence tendencies:** short. Sometimes very short. Russian syntactic residue permitted sparingly (dropped articles, terminal placement) but must be earned by the moment.
- **Silences:** the longest of any POV. Anton does not think aloud. His POV should feel like a room with the lights off except where his hands are working.
- **Dialogue tics:** minimal words. When he speaks, others listen. Occasional dry aside in Register B that lands because it is the only thing he has said in an hour.
- **In Register B:** Katya's cello playing from another room. Tea rather than coffee. He watches Jack over the rim of a cup.

## Reese Long

**Registers:** A and B.
**Charm register:** B (kinetic, irreverent); A (when reconnaissance is going right).

- **Notices first:** surfaces, angles, seams, load points, where a body could fit, where it couldn't but she could.
- **Measures time by:** breath, grip fatigue, the count in her head, how many minutes since her ankle last twinged.
- **Native metaphors:** movement. Torque, catch, release, drift, line.
- **Never in words:** the word *hurt*. The phrase *ankle problem*. Anything that admits limitation.
- **Sentence tendencies:** kinetic. Verbs first. Present-tense feel even in past tense. Fragments allowed in her interior because her interior moves faster than complete sentences.
- **Silences:** she goes completely still when genuinely frightened. Otherwise she is moving.
- **Dialogue tics:** deflects with a joke, especially when the joke is on herself. Calls Jack "boss" only when she is not okay.
- **In Register B:** climbs things she shouldn't. Sits on counters. Steals food off other people's plates.
- **Book One arc constraint:** hides the ankle. Her POV must place the ankle in the reader's peripheral vision without her ever letting it into her own conscious foreground.

## Avery Bentley

**Registers:** B (rare) and C.
**Charm register:** C in a quieter register than Jack's.

- **Notices first:** what a person is not saying. The pauses in a conversation. What a room's furniture claims and whether the claim is true.
- **Measures time by:** what Stephen used to do at this hour.
- **Native metaphors:** literary and observational. She reads people the way she reads a paragraph.
- **Never in words:** the word *widow*. Anything sentimental about Stephen. Any admission that she is frightened.
- **Sentence tendencies:** composed, careful, slightly literary. Sentences slightly longer than Jack's. Grief renders as precision, not fragmentation.
- **Silences:** used deliberately. Avery has learned in seven weeks that saying less gets her more.
- **Dialogue tics:** answers questions with questions when she does not yet trust the asker. Uses first names carefully.
- **Book One arc constraint:** she is not a client to be protected. Her POV, when used, should establish her as a person Jack underestimates at his cost.

## Stephen Bentley — Prologue only

**Register:** A (dominant); a brief C-adjacent opening in the lobby.
**Charm register:** none.

- **Notices first:** procedure. Badges, cameras, indicator lights, camera indicators that are dark when they should not be. Building details he has walked past for years and never registered.
- **Measures time by:** the countdown Chelsea has given him and the minutes remaining on the access window.
- **Native metaphors:** accounting. Balance, reconciliation, entry, ledger, close.
- **Never in words:** the word *dead*. The word *murder*. Avery's name spoken in his head as a plea.
- **Sentence tendencies:** short, procedural, controlled. Fear renders through physical detail: sweat, shake, blurred photo, breath.
- **Silences:** used as concealment. Stephen listens for footsteps more than he thinks.
- **Dialogue tics:** the practiced cover story. "Quarter-end." "Weaver wants the exhibits by seven." Cover fluency is a character trait, not a plot device.

## Addison Moody

**Registers:** C dominant; occasional A when authorizing an operation from her own end.
**Charm register:** C. Untrusted by the reader.

- **Notices first:** utility. What a person is good for. What they cost. Where their loyalty terminates.
- **Measures time by:** deal cycles, client windows, and the length of a well-run meeting.
- **Native metaphors:** aesthetic. She thinks in composition, line, proportion, and taste. She reads people the way a collector reads a room.
- **Never in words:** any word that admits the criminal structure. Her own thoughts euphemize.
- **Sentence tendencies:** elegant, precise, slightly cool. Longer than Jack's. She takes pleasure in a well-formed sentence and the prose lets her.
- **Silences:** she uses silence as reward and withdrawal both.
- **Dialogue tics:** first names early. Compliments that are also assessments. Never raises her voice.
- **Book One and forward constraint:** the reader knows her role before Jack does. Her POV must not tip that knowledge through interior narration; it should carry it through composure.

## Hattie Baker

**Register:** C dominant; A when she is on scene.
**Charm register:** none. She carries professional weight.

- **Notices first:** inconsistency. Timelines that do not close, receipts that do not reconcile, a person who is too calm, a person who is too careful.
- **Measures time by:** case cycles, statute periods, chain of custody, how long a lead has been open.
- **Native metaphors:** procedural and evidentiary. Predicate, proffer, thread, chain, custody, standard.
- **Never in words:** any admission that she suspects Jack without proof. The word *intuition* about her own reasoning.
- **Sentence tendencies:** careful, complete, slightly formal. No fragments. She does not permit them in her own thought.
- **Silences:** patient. Hattie's silence is a professional instrument. Interviewees fill it and hurt themselves.
- **Dialogue tics:** courteous. Uses titles. Never confrontational until she is closing.

## Card maintenance

- Any chapter that introduces a new sustained POV requires a card here before acceptance.
- Cards are updated only through explicit canon change (see `canon/01_CANON_AUTHORITY_AND_CHANGE_CONTROL.md`).
- A voice-drift finding in the acceptance audit is grounds for revising the manuscript, not the card.
