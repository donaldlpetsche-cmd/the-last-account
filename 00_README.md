# Jack Mallory Project Archive

**Export date:** August 5, 2026  
**Project status:** Active development  
**Current Book One:** *The Last Account*  
**Working franchise name:** *Jack Mallory Series*

This archive converts the current project canon, planning decisions, continuity controls, and drafting rules into portable Markdown files.

## Authority order

When two sources conflict, use this order:

1. A direct user approval or correction made after the August 3, 2026 series reset.
2. The approved August 5, 2026 Book One chapter map.
3. The current Series Bible and Character Bible in this archive.
4. The live continuity ledgers for the active manuscript.
5. The Showrunner Notebook, which is non-canon unless promoted in writing.
6. Any earlier chat, draft, uploaded scene, outline, or manuscript. These are quarantined.

## Canon labels

- **LOCKED CANON:** Approved and binding until deliberately changed.
- **CANON:** Established and usable, but may still receive non-destructive detail.
- **PROVISIONAL:** Planned direction that has not been fully locked.
- **UNRESOLVED:** No answer has been approved. Do not invent one during drafting.
- **QUARANTINED:** Superseded material that must not influence current drafting.

## Archive contents

- `canon/` — series premise, characters, organizations, rules, and world state.
- `season_one/` — the ten-book season architecture and unresolved season planning.
- `book_01_the_last_account/` — master outline, continuity ledgers, and chapter working files.
- `workflow/` — prose standard and mandatory drafting/continuity procedure.
- `quarantine/` — legacy-file manifest and excluded concepts.
- `admin/` — changelog, open questions, status, and file hashes.

## Important manuscript limitation

Current-canon prose for the prologue and early chapters was drafted inside project chats, not saved as accessible source files. This archive does **not** fabricate or paraphrase those passages as though they were the original manuscript. It preserves their approved plot state, chapter status, evidence chains, and continuity decisions. The chat remains the source for any verbatim prose not separately exported.

## Working locally

Treat this folder as the project root. Before drafting a chapter, open:

1. `canon/02_SERIES_BIBLE.md`
2. `canon/03_CHARACTER_BIBLE.md`
3. `book_01_the_last_account/01_MASTER_CHAPTER_MAP.md`
4. The chapter file in `book_01_the_last_account/chapter_files/`
5. All continuity files in `book_01_the_last_account/continuity/`

After drafting, complete the audit in `workflow/02_MANDATORY_CONTINUITY_WORKFLOW.md` before accepting the chapter or starting another.
