## The operating base

A working warehouse on the Chelsea waterfront. Purchased cash years ago and held in the name of a long-standing alias built by Nina; the alias predates the property acquisition and has independent filing, credit, tax, and utility history sufficient to survive routine records inspection. Jack Mallory does not appear anywhere on the title chain, and no legal entity connected to his advisory practice is on record against the address.

The building looks industrial from the outside and is kept that way. Loading dock, freight elevator, fenced yard, vehicle bay large enough to accept the Suburban and close the door behind it. Interior built out for operations: tech room for Nina, secure records storage, gear lockers, an occasional workbench for Anton, open floor for Reese to rig practice climbs, small kitchen, two couches. No pretensions to living space.

**Who knows the address:** the permanent five only. The base predates Anton and Reese joining the crew; each of them was brought there for the first time as a deliberate moment of trust. The address is not shared with clients, referrals, family members (including Betty), or professional contacts.
