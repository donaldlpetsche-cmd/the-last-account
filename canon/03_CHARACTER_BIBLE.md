# Character Bible

## Jack Mallory

**Status:** LOCKED CANON with listed unresolved fields  
**Age at Book One:** 43  
**Origin:** Boston

### Background

- Raised poor in Boston.
- Mother died when Jack was approximately six or seven.
- Father deteriorated into drinking and emotional absence.
- Older brother Matthew, roughly ten years older, was Jack's primary source of safety and then left at sixteen or seventeen because he felt trapped.
- Jack experienced Matthew's departure as abandonment.
- Jack ran away at fourteen.
- Books, libraries, and Stan's used bookstore became refuge and education.
- Stan caught Jack stealing a book Jack wanted to understand and made him work off the debt.
- Jack learned through observation and access rather than a polished childhood path.
- Attended an ordinary or regional undergraduate institution on scholarship.
- Graduated from Harvard Law.
- Was once a licensed attorney and is no longer licensed.
- Built a reputation advising wealthy families and closely held enterprises in estates, acquisitions, private crises, and difficult special situations.
- An early breakthrough for a wealthy family generated referrals into elite networks.
- He remains available for legitimate confidential consulting.
- He built substantial personal wealth through legitimate work and side operations. The exact wealth origin and composition remain unresolved.

### Present identity

Jack is a private, confidential special-situations adviser whose clients understand that he solves problems other professionals cannot. He can move credibly through elite rooms without belonging emotionally to them.

### Core capabilities

- Systems-level insight.
- Legal and institutional reasoning.
- Planning and objective design.
- Social engineering and negotiation.
- Reading incentives, power, and hidden dependencies.
- Building teams and assigning authority.
- Remaining functional under pressure.

### Emotional state

- The permanent crew is his family.
- He has had little sustained romantic experience and is missing a long-term intimate relationship.
- He is at the peak of his ability and is not preoccupied with aging.
- He often converts love into planning, control, and protection.
- His Book One failure is withholding mission-critical danger from the crew, denying them informed choice and contributing to Reese's injury and the fracture with Marcus.

### Unresolved

- Exact undergraduate school and field.
- Exact circumstances of losing or surrendering his law license.
- Exact source, size, and structure of his wealth.
- Current residence and primary operating facility details.
- Father's current life, sobriety, remarriage, or later respectability.
- The long-term resolution with Matthew.

## Nina Rodriquez

**Status:** CANON  
**Age at Book One:** 35  
**Role:** Systems

### Background

- Hispanic.
- Grew up in California, possibly in a small agricultural or migrant community.
- Orphaned at approximately twelve and moved through foster homes.
- No formal higher education.
- Self-taught.
- Has spent much of adulthood using forged credentials and assumed identities.
- First person Jack brought into the crew.
- Met Jack when she was approximately nineteen and he was twenty-seven.
- Has worked with him for roughly sixteen years.

### Capabilities

- Surveillance and counter-surveillance support.
- Networks and communications.
- Electronic access.
- Identity construction.
- Intelligence synthesis.
- Financial and records analysis.
- Technical planning and live operational control.

### Personality

- Tech nerd.
- Slightly awkward, guarded, sarcastic.
- Devoted to the found family and shows it in her own visible but indirect way.
- Single; relationships tend to be casual, distant, or largely digital.

### Fatal flaw and fear

Nina believes information is safer than trust. She uses knowledge and alternate identities to preserve control. She fears powerlessness while loved ones are harmed and fears losing identity or belonging.

She is field-capable but materially weaker when technology, data, or communications fail.

### Stress tells

- Technical speech accelerates.
- Sarcasm sharpens.
- Pulls sleeves over her hands.
- Dismantles electronics.
- Avoids eye contact when receiving sincere affection.
- Becomes quiet when truly afraid.

## Marcus Lowell

**Status:** CANON  
**Age at Book One:** 46  
**Role:** Security  
**Physical:** Black; approximately 6'2" and 215 pounds; broad-shouldered and conditioned

### Background

- Served approximately thirteen years in the United States Marine Corps.
- Force Recon background.
- Left honorably for family.
- Final enlisted rank is unresolved between Staff Sergeant and Gunnery Sergeant.
- Joined the crew one to two years after Nina, through executive or corporate security work.
- Owns Lowell Security Group as a legitimate cover and operating business.

### Capabilities

- Tactical planning.
- Physical security and executive protection.
- Transport and precision driving.
- Surveillance and counter-surveillance.
- Infiltration and extraction.
- Threat assessment.
- Weapons.
- Tactical medicine.
- Logistics.

### Personality

Calm, disciplined, observant, patient, protective, practical, dryly funny, and emotionally contained.

### Family

- Wife: Betty Lowell, 45, school counselor. She knows the real nature of Marcus's work.
- Son: Marcus Lowell Jr., 15, baseball.
- Daughter: Gabriella “Gabs” Lowell, 12, interested in musicals.
- The Lowell home is the crew's primary family gathering place.
- The children are family, never operational assets.

### Fatal flaw and fear

Marcus cannot leave the perimeter. Vigilance becomes control, surveillance, emotional unavailability, and inability to relax. He fears bringing danger home or being absent when his family needs him.

In Book One, Jack's withholding violates Marcus's command trust and informed-risk standard.

## Anton Rominov

**Status:** CANON  
**Age at Book One:** 62  
**Role:** Entry

### Background

- Born in the Soviet Union.
- Immigrated at approximately sixteen or seventeen.
- Son of a locksmith.
- Master locksmith and mechanical-entry specialist.
- Former CIA asset.
- Runs a real locksmith and security shop.
- Widower.
- His connection to the intelligence world never fully ended.

### Capabilities

- Locks, safes, and vaults.
- Antique and modern mechanical systems.
- Restoration hardware.
- Precision entry and restoration.
- Tamper systems.
- Quiet, disciplined physical work.

### Personality and flaw

Fiercely loyal, terse, exact, patient, and self-contained. His loyalty can become self-erasure. He has a history of accepting obligations without preserving his own agency.

### Family

- Daughter: Katya Rominov, 16.
- Katya is serious and a committed cellist.
- Anton's central fear is that his past will reach Katya.

### Long arc

Anton must increasingly choose his present family over old debts and choose truth and agency over conditioned loyalty.

## Reese Long

**Status:** CANON  
**Age at Book One:** 26  
**Role:** Access  
**Position in crew:** Youngest and newest permanent member

### Background

- Former Olympic gymnastics hopeful.
- A severe ankle injury ended her Olympic path after years of pushing performance limits.
- Rehabilitation led to climbing, parkour, urban exploration, and movement through inaccessible structures.
- Creates art in hard-to-reach places.
- Signature mark is a stylized peanut butter cup.
- Raised by loving but intensely structured parents whose family life revolved around gymnastics.
- Her parents remained supportive after the injury, but the relationship became strained when she rejected prescribed replacement paths.

### Appearance and manner

- Approximately 5'1", although she claims more.
- Compact and muscular.
- Short hair.
- Casual, functional clothing.
- Carefree, restless, expressive.

### Capabilities

- Reconnaissance.
- Climbing and confined movement.
- Physical infiltration.
- Improvisation.
- Reading structural access routes.
- Operating where conventional entry is impossible.

### Fatal flaw and fear

Reese believes capability eliminates valid fear. She hides pain and instability, resists retreat, and takes risks to prove she belongs. Her deepest fear is physical limitation, replacement, or becoming unnecessary.

### Relationships

- The crew has embraced her as loved family.
- Jack relationship: younger-sibling dynamic. She admires and challenges him and resists being managed.
- Book One uses her concealed ankle instability and Jack's concealed stakes to trigger a major trust fracture.

### Stress tells

- Restless movement, climbing, or balancing.
- Louder and more dismissive when hiding pain.
- Taps or flexes the injured ankle.
- Becomes completely still when genuinely frightened.

## Addison Moody

**Status:** CANON  
**Age at Book One:** 52  
**Role:** Founder and CEO of AM Strategic Advisory; hidden Season One adversary; emerging romantic adversary

### Public identity

Addison leads AM Strategic Advisory, LLC, publicly known as AM Advisory. The Washington-based firm is legitimate and respected. It handles crisis management, litigation intelligence, corporate investigations, cyber response, reputation, political risk, and security.

### Hidden identity

Addison leads a compartmentalized criminal special-matters structure within or behind the firm. It manufactures outcomes for powerful clients through:

- Surveillance.
- Cyber intrusion.
- Coercion.
- Bribery.
- Evidence manipulation.
- Legal and political pressure.
- Murder.

Addison authorizes outcomes and treats people as expendable when required by the objective.

### Jack relationship

- The reader knows Addison's identity and role before Jack.
- Jack initially knows only the accomplished public adviser and senses danger.
- Addison suspects Jack contributed to the Book One failure but does not know his crew or method.
- Attraction begins at the end of Book One or in Book Two.
- The emotion is immediate and genuine despite opposing sides.
- The relationship develops while both conceal major truths.

## Book One supporting cast

### Stephen Bentley
A Weaver, Newman & Gaines employee who discovers the handwritten ledger, photographs it, is framed for an eighteen-million-dollar theft, and is killed in a staged carbon-monoxide death.

### Avery Bentley
Stephen's widow. Brings Jack the accusation, voicemail, notes, photograph, and old key. She does not know about the gala or ledger when she first approaches him.

### Tomas Weaver
Senior figure at Weaver, Newman & Gaines, LLC. Book One's visible principal antagonist. Hired the outside organization to frame Stephen, suppress the discovery, destroy the ledger, and eliminate witnesses.

### Chelsea Boone
Stephen's internal colleague. Leaves a deliberate audit inconsistency as a signal, later confirms the ledger and cleanup plan, and becomes a threatened witness.

### Rylan Adams
Security director and mid-level associate of the hidden organization. Not part of Addison's inner circle. Implements surveillance, Stephen's staging, records control, gala cleanup, and the attempted fire.

### Marley Nielsen
Former Jack client and referral source connecting Avery to Jack. Exact prior case details remain unresolved.

### Owen Price
Manager associated with the Quincy security firm used to surveil Avery. Previously worked under Rylan Adams.

### Ortega
Person who discovers Stephen's car in the Weaver Newman garage and contacts Rylan before calling 911.
