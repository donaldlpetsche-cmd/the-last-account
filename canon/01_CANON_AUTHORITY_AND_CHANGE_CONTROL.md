# Canon Authority and Change Control

## Reset boundary

The current Jack Mallory canon begins with the August 3, 2026 franchise reset and the decisions that followed it. Earlier plots, biographies, manuscripts, and scene files are not source material unless a later decision explicitly reintroduced an element.

Retaining a character name does not retain that character's old biography, relationships, history, dialogue, or plot actions.

## Canon management rules

1. The Series Bible contains canon only.
2. The Showrunner Notebook contains possibilities, discarded concepts, unresolved questions, and future options.
3. No major canon may be invented silently during drafting.
4. New facts created by a chapter become canon only after the chapter passes continuity review.
5. Corrections must be propagated through the chapter map, Series Bible, continuity ledger, object/evidence chain, and knowledge matrix.
6. A later explicit user correction supersedes an earlier approval.
7. When an old file conflicts with current canon, the old file loses automatically.

## Information discipline

A character may act only on information the character has legitimately obtained. Reader knowledge, antagonist knowledge, crew knowledge, and individual character knowledge must remain separate.

Book One's central discipline:

- The reader learns Addison Moody controls the hidden organization before Jack does.
- Jack does not identify Addison's hidden role in Book One.
- Addison suspects Jack disrupted the operation but does not know the permanent crew or how it functions.
- Hattie suspects Jack is connected to unusual outcomes but has no proof and does not identify the permanent crew in Book One.
