# The Last Account — Master Chapter Map

**Status:** Approved map  
**Structure:** Prologue + 28 numbered chapters + coda  
**Target allocation:** 57,300 words from the approved line items. The earlier map header stated 57,200; the 100-word arithmetic discrepancy remains open and no chapter target has been silently altered.  
**Primary viewpoint:** Jack, with limited controlled secondary viewpoints

## Prologue — The Account

**POV:** Stephen (Part I), Chelsea Boone (Part II)
**Register:** A dominant throughout; C for Chelsea's office setting
**Target:** ~2,000 words (Stephen ~700; Chelsea ~1,300)

Part I — Stephen: Cold open inside the original vault. The access panel outside begins to cycle. Stephen slits the briefcase lining with a fingernail, jams the old key through the gap and presses the fabric flat. He pockets his phone with a drafted message to Avery and one attached ledger image, both unsent (no signal). He walks toward the door composed. Rylan intercepts. Cover fails in one exchange. Stephen tries to break past and is knocked unconscious. The photographs die on his phone. The key survives in the lining.

Part II — Chelsea: The next morning at WNG. Chelsea has been monitoring a data-only cryptic status utility from home during Stephen's entry. She never had audio or video. A prearranged one-word check-in from Stephen by 6:15 a.m. never arrived. At her desk from 7:30 onward she pretends to work while replaying what she watched: the countdown, the MOVE alert she pushed when a vault security-policy change fired from an admin console, the session terminating fourteen seconds later without his sign-off. The word *suicide* moves through the floor by mid-morning. She understands what actually happened, understands she is now the only person who does, and chooses silence. She does not signal. She does not contact Avery.

**Closing hook:** Chelsea watches Rylan cross the floor toward Tomas's office at 9:47 a.m. and does nothing.

## Avery Bentley

**POV:** Jack  
**Target:** 2,300 words

Avery explains the eighteen-million-dollar embezzlement accusation against Stephen and his death. She gives Jack the voicemail, notes, a photograph, and the old key found in Stephen’s briefcase lining. Avery does not know about the gala or the handwritten ledger. Jack agrees only to conduct a preliminary review.

**Closing hook:** The account is not money.

## Eighteen Million Dollars

**POV:** Nina  
**Target:** 2,100 words

Using the forensic copy and records already supplied through Avery, Nina independently locates the report and hidden workbook fields. Approval records were created after Stephen’s death, and the money remained inside the Weaver Newman/client orbit. Surveillance of Avery is traced through a café camera to a Quincy security firm managed by Owen Price, who previously worked under Rylan. Jack has not formally accepted the case yet.

**Closing hook:** Someone built a theft around Stephen Bentley’s name.

## A Convenient Death

**POV:** Jack  
**Target:** 2,100 words

Marcus reconstructs Stephen’s final movements. His car entered a surface lot at 7:36 a.m., was manually released at 1:12 a.m. by a driver who was not Stephen, moved three-tenths of a mile, shut off at 1:19, restarted at 1:47, and ran until discovery at 5:41. Ortega called Rylan before 911. The scene was orderly and cleaned, and Rylan’s report classified Stephen as adverse before discovery. Stephen had future plans and was behaving like a frightened man, not a suicidal one.

**Closing hook:** Stephen was not preparing to die.

## The Key

**POV:** Anton  
**Target:** 1,900 words

Anton identifies the old key as belonging to the Boston Mercantile Trust’s original safe-deposit or document-control hardware. It cannot open the main vault. It opens an internal cabinet retained inside the restored vault complex.

**Closing hook:** Whatever Stephen wanted found is still inside their building.

## The Referral

**POV:** Jack  
**Target:** 1,900 words

Jack meets privately with Marley Nielsen and establishes why Avery was referred to him, why discretion matters, and how quickly ordinary inquiry activated surveillance. The referral becomes part of Jack’s vetting rather than proof of the case.

**Closing hook:** Taking the review may already have activated the watcher.

## The Engagement

**POV:** Jack  
**Target:** 2,000 words

Jack tells Avery the evidence does not support Stephen stealing the money. He formally accepts the case. The objective becomes recovering what Stephen found and identifying who killed him, not merely clearing an accounting discrepancy.

**Closing hook:** Jack schedules the meeting with Tomas Weaver.

## Tomas Weaver

**POV:** Jack  
**Target:** 2,300 words

Jack meets Tomas in the Boston Mercantile Trust Building. Tomas presents as composed and sympathetic, describes Stephen as a trusted employee who betrayed everyone, and offers Avery a confidential settlement if she ends the challenge. Rylan attends under a sensitive-records security pretext and watches Jack. Tomas invokes Jack’s old disciplinary history.

**Closing hook:** Tomas investigated Jack before the meeting.

## Federal Interest

**POV:** Hattie  
**Target:** 1,700 words

Working an unrelated financial investigation involving a Weaver Newman client, Hattie notices Jack in visitor records and recognizes his old disciplinary matter. Tomas gives a technically responsive but overly polished answer about Stephen’s access. Hattie requests original audit material.

**Closing hook:** Counsel says the relevant records are privileged and no longer held in original form.

## The Gala

**POV:** Nina  
**Target:** 2,100 words

Nina investigates building operations and independently discovers the foundation gala. She finds temporary system connections and unusual contractor activity around the event and concludes the gala creates both cover and a destruction window.

**Closing hook:** Whatever Stephen hid is likely leaving that night.

## The Service Side

**POV:** Reese  
**Target:** 1,900 words

Reese studies old building plans and identifies an abandoned counterweight shaft leading to the vault’s rear service area. During a test climb, her ankle briefly fails. She hides it and marks the route with her peanut-butter-cup sketch.

**Closing hook:** She can reach the vault from a side the current coverage does not see.

## The Missing Colleague

**POV:** Jack  
**Target:** 2,000 words

Jack identifies Chelsea Boone through Stephen’s final workdays. Chelsea left a deliberate audit inconsistency as a signal: she wants someone to know the report is false without admitting that she knows.

**Closing hook:** The error is intentional communication.

## Proof of Life

**POV:** Jack  
**Target:** 2,000 words

Jack reaches Chelsea indirectly, proves Avery is uncompromised, and arranges a controlled meeting. Chelsea fears that contact will get Avery killed.

**Closing hook:** They are moving it during the gala.

## Chelsea Boone

**POV:** Jack  
**Target:** 2,400 words

Chelsea confirms the handwritten ledger is in the original vault, Stephen was framed, and the gala is being used to destroy the evidence. Jack protects Chelsea’s identity from the crew while extracting the operational facts.

**Closing hook:** Stephen’s key opens the ledger cabinet.

## The Plan

**POV:** Jack  
**Target:** 2,300 words

Jack enters openly as a donor adviser. Nina takes event systems. Anton uses a service identity. Reese handles the shaft bypass. Marcus controls surveillance, transport, extraction, and abort authority. Jack withholds Chelsea’s identity. Marcus establishes the abort threshold.

**Closing hook:** Marcus asks whether Jack’s source is still inside the building.

## Sunday Dinner

**POV:** Jack  
**Target:** 2,100 words

The Lowell home shows the crew as family with Betty, Marcus Jr., Gabs, Katya, Nina, Anton, Reese, Marcus, and Jack. Operational preparation sits beside ordinary domestic life. New intelligence establishes that the ledger will transfer immediately after the gala.

**Closing hook:** The timetable collapses forward.

## The Boston Mercantile Trust

**POV:** Jack  
**Target:** 1,800 words

The gala begins in the restored banking hall. The team enters in assigned roles and the operation starts cleanly.

**Closing hook:** Jack sees Hattie Baker speaking with Tomas.

## Unscheduled Guests

**POV:** Hattie  
**Target:** 1,500 words

Hattie discreetly questions a Weaver Newman client during the gala, notices Jack, and watches Rylan tighten access to restricted floors.

**Closing hook:** An interviewee leaves through a private exit.

## The Shaft

**POV:** Reese  
**Target:** 1,600 words

Reese descends the narrow abandoned shaft, finds recent blockage and instability, and reports normal despite increasing ankle pain. The bypass point has been altered.

**Closing hook:** Her device fits, but she must cross an unsupported gap.

## Moving the Book

**POV:** Jack  
**Target:** 1,700 words

Jack pressures Tomas in a way that forces the ledger to move early. Rylan accelerates security. Chelsea and two accounting employees are ordered to the archive level. Service elevators are remotely restricted.

**Closing hook:** Chelsea reports fire suppression disabled below the ballroom.

## Acceptable Losses

**POV:** Jack  
**Target:** 1,700 words

Jack recognizes the cleanup plan: destroy the ledger, trap Chelsea and the accounting employees, stage an electrical fire, and evacuate the public while lower-level personnel remain exposed. Jack withholds the full discovery and tells the crew only that the transfer accelerated. Nina detects unexplained building-control commands. Marcus loses another exit.

**Closing hook:** Marcus says the abort threshold has been crossed.

## Abort

**POV:** Marcus  
**Target:** 1,600 words

Federal agents are inside, security patterns shift, communications degrade, Reese has the only viable route, and no clean extraction remains. Marcus recommends abort. Jack refuses while still withholding mission-critical information. Marcus follows because the team is already committed.

**Closing hook:** This is your call. What happens next belongs to you.

## The Fall

**POV:** Reese  
**Target:** 1,500 words

Reese crosses the unsupported gap and installs the bypass. Her ankle fails, she falls and sustains a serious leg injury, becomes trapped, minimizes the damage, and holds the mechanism open while smoke begins entering the archive level.

**Closing hook:** The route works only while Reese keeps holding it.

## The Vault

**POV:** Anton  
**Target:** 1,600 words

Anton uses Stephen’s key on the internal document cabinet and recovers the ledger. He recognizes an intelligence-influenced tamper system. Reese maintains the bypass while injured, and Nina suppresses electronic alarms. Smoke and fire threaten Chelsea and the employees.

**Closing hook:** Nina determines the fire is controlled, not accidental.

## What Jack Knew

**POV:** Marcus  
**Target:** 1,600 words

Anton has the ledger, Nina fights the controls, Marcus moves to free Reese, and Jack moves toward Chelsea. The fire reveals that Jack knew people were targets and withheld the risk. The crew fracture becomes explicit.

**Closing hook:** We finish this conversation after I bring her home.

## Rylan Adams

**POV:** Jack  
**Target:** 1,800 words

Rylan intercepts Jack in the archive corridor. Jack draws out admissions about Stephen, Chelsea, the fire, and Tomas’s orders. The confrontation turns physical. Jack disables Rylan rather than executing him.

**Closing hook:** The door locks with Chelsea inside.

## The Extraction

**POV:** Nina / tightly controlled rotating focus  
**Target:** 2,000 words

Nina restores suppression and an emergency route. Jack reaches Chelsea and the employees. Marcus frees injured Reese. Anton transfers the ledger. Reese guides Marcus despite the injury. Hattie notices the public evacuation is leaving lower-level workers behind. Nina distributes authenticated ledger pages and supporting evidence.

**Closing hook:** At the ambulances, Jack sees Marcus carrying Reese. Marcus does not look at him.

## The Truth About Stephen

**POV:** Avery, then Hattie  
**Target:** 2,200 words

The ledger and digital records expose the framing. Chelsea explains the system, and the Rylan and fire evidence supports deliberate cleanup. Avery receives the truth about Stephen. Reese undergoes surgery. Hattie reviews the evidence and reopens Jack’s file.

**Closing hook:** Hattie writes: MALLORY, JACK.

## What Family Means

**POV:** Jack  
**Target:** 2,500 words

Aftermath and emotional accounting. Jack faces Reese’s injury and Marcus’s anger. The external victory does not erase the informed-consent failure. Avery and Chelsea survive the operation and Stephen’s name is restored. The crew remains family, but trust must be rebuilt through future conduct rather than a clean speech or instant forgiveness.

**Closing hook:** The season threat remains outside Jack’s knowledge.

## Coda — Not Yet

**POV:** Addison  
**Target:** 1,300 words

Addison reviews the failed special matter. AM Advisory built Stephen’s accusation, approved his death, and authorized destruction of the ledger and witnesses. Jack is the leading explanation. Addison cannot determine how he did it and does not know the permanent crew. The reader learns her role before Jack.

**Closing hook:** Not yet.
