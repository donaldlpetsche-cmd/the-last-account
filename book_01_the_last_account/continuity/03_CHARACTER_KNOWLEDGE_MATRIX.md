## Critical information gates

### Avery
- Must not know gala date or ledger before Nina and the crew discover them.
- Must not communicate investigative findings directly to Nina unless specifically established.
- Chapter 1 photograph is not a ledger image (see Object and Evidence Chain).

### Chelsea
- Reader meets her in the prologue (Part II).
- Between the prologue and Chapter 13 she takes no on-page action. Offscreen references only.
- She does not contact Avery. Locked.
- She does not signal again after the prologue. The audit inconsistency Jack's crew will find was placed eight weeks earlier.
- Her Chapter 13 re-engagement is initiated by Jack, not by her.

### Jack
- Learns fire/witness plan before Marcus.
- Withholds Chelsea's identity during planning.
- Withholds full witness danger in Chapters 20–21.
- Does not learn Addison's hidden role in Book One.

### Crew
- Knows transfer accelerated.
- Does not initially know Jack has concluded Chelsea and two employees are intended fire victims.
- Learns the truth through the operation, causing fracture.

### Hattie
- Can suspect Jack.
- Cannot identify Nina, Marcus, Anton, or Reese as the permanent crew in Book One.
- Ends with an active or reopened file, not a solved conspiracy.

### Addison
- Can identify Jack as leading suspicion.
- Cannot know how many permanent operators he has, their identities, or their division of labor.
