# The Last Account — Object and Evidence Chain

## Old key

1. Originally belongs to retained Boston Mercantile Trust document-control hardware.
2. Used by Stephen to open the internal cabinet in the original vault.
3. Hidden by Stephen in his briefcase lining.
4. Found by Avery after his death.
5. Given by Avery to Jack during Chapter 1.
6. Examined by Anton in Chapter 4.
7. Retained securely by crew until gala operation.
8. Used by Anton in Chapter 23 to open the ledger cabinet.

**Do not:** allow the key to open the main vault, appear from another source, or be known to Tomas as recovered unless a chapter establishes that discovery.

## Handwritten ledger

1. Stored in internal document-control cabinet inside original vault.
2. Photographed by Stephen in the prologue.
3. Remains physically in building after Stephen's death.
4. Scheduled for movement and destruction during gala.
5. Recovered physically by Anton in Chapter 23.
6. Transferred during extraction.
7. Authenticated pages and supporting records distributed by Nina in Chapter 26.
8. Used with digital records to expose Stephen's framing and the purchased-outcomes system.

## Stephen's photographs

Stephen photographs ledger pages in the prologue. The exact survival, device, upload, and later recovery chain is not fully locked in the available project record.

**Continuity rule:** Do not assume Avery possesses the ledger photographs. Chapter 1's “photograph” must be identified in manuscript continuity before it is treated as a ledger image.
