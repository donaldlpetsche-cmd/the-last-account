# The Last Account — Master Continuity Ledger

## Timeline convention

Exact calendar dates are not yet locked. Use relative time until a master calendar is approved.

### Prior to Stephen's final week

- Edward Hollister, retired senior partner at WNG, dies of natural causes approximately six months before the prologue night.
- His office and files enter the associate wind-down rotation. Stephen is one of the associates assigned to client-file transitions.
- Chelsea Boone, during her own involvement in the wind-down, recovers the old key from Hollister's office.
- Approximately eight weeks before the prologue night, Chelsea places a deliberate audit inconsistency in a report as bait for anyone attentive enough to see it. Stephen sees it and approaches her. They begin quietly collaborating. Over the following weeks they reconstruct enough of the underlying transaction pattern to conclude the operational record lives in the original vault's document-control cabinet. They build the entry plan together.
- Chelsea places the recovered key in a yellow envelope with fresh adhesive beneath the bottom drawer of Hollister's desk. Chelsea and Stephen never discuss the key directly at any point. Everything else about the plan they discuss openly. The silence is a narrow, deliberate forensic choice: if Stephen is caught and questioned, he can truthfully say he found the key in a deceased partner's desk without Chelsea's name entering the account.
- Approximately six days before the prologue night, Stephen finds the envelope in the ordinary course of his work and understands the signal.
- Chelsea and Stephen prearrange a benign one-word check-in text Stephen will send to Chelsea by 6:15 a.m. the morning after the entry to confirm he got out.

### Seven weeks before Avery meets Jack — night of the entry (Prologue Part I)

- Stephen becomes frightened during his final week.
- Changes parking and routine.
- Checks the alarm.
- Makes calls outside his ordinary pattern.
- Continues making future plans.
- Chelsea creates a temporary access and camera window through a maintenance utility loaded on a legitimately provisioned facilities credential. The link is data-only cryptic telemetry with short text messaging in either direction. It carries no audio and no video. On session termination the link and its logs auto-purge from Chelsea's end.
- Stephen enters the Boston Mercantile Trust after hours.
- Uses legitimate WNG credentials, Tomas Weaver's code, the biometric palm reader, and the old key.
- Descends via the preserved bank-officers' stair from the executive corridor directly to the vault level.
- Photographs the handwritten ledger (non-sequential order, two frames per page per Chelsea's protocol).
- Drafts a message to Avery on his phone (locked wording per object chain). Attaches one ledger image. Does not send. No signal in the vault.
- Chelsea, monitoring from home, sees a vault security-policy change fire from an admin console. She pushes MOVE. SECURITY CHANGE. into Stephen's session.
- The vault access panel begins to cycle from outside.
- Stephen slits the briefcase lining with a fingernail, jams the key through the gap, presses the fabric flat.
- Pockets the phone.
- Walks toward the door composed.
- Rylan enters. Cover exchange fails in one round.
- Stephen tries to break past. He is knocked unconscious three or four strides from the door.
- Chelsea's session terminates fourteen seconds after her MOVE alert, without Stephen's sign-off.
- Stephen's phone is taken into Rylan's custody. The photographs and the drafted message die with the device.
- Stephen's car leaves the surface lot at 1:12 a.m. with an unidentified driver.
- Car is staged in the Weaver Newman garage.
- Official cause becomes carbon monoxide.
- Vehicle is discovered at 5:41 a.m.
- Ortega contacts Rylan before 911.

### Morning after the entry (Prologue Part II)

- Chelsea's prearranged 6:15 a.m. check-in text does not arrive.
- Chelsea arrives at WNG at her ordinary hour and takes her desk from approximately 7:30 a.m.
- The news of Stephen's death moves through the floor by mid-morning. The word *suicide* is understood in the pauses.
- Chelsea holds professional composure. She replays the previous night's telemetry in her head and understands what happened.
- She takes no action. She does not signal. She does not contact Avery. She continues to work.
- At 9:47 a.m. she watches Rylan cross the floor toward Tomas Weaver's office.

### Chelsea's posture, Chapters 2 through 12

- Chelsea goes still. No new signals, no reach-outs, no anomalies. She does her job.
- The audit inconsistency she placed eight weeks earlier remains in the file. It is the thread Jack's crew will eventually find.
- One or two glancing offscreen references in Chapters 2 through 12 (a name on a wind-down team list; a mention on a distribution) keep her in the reader's peripheral vision. She does not have a scene.
- Chapter 13 is her first re-engagement. Jack finds her. She does not surface herself.

### Day 1 — Avery approaches Jack

- Avery provides voicemail, notes, photograph, and old key.
- Jack accepts only a preliminary review.
- Avery does not know about the gala or the ledger.

### Early review

- Nina independently locates the report through Avery's existing data package.
- Hidden workbook fields show post-death approvals.
- The eighteen million dollars remains in the Weaver Newman/client orbit.
- Avery surveillance is traced through Owen Price to Rylan.
- Marcus establishes Stephen's death was staged.
- Anton identifies the key's function.
- Jack accepts the case.

### Investigation and operation planning

- Tomas offers a confidential settlement and reveals knowledge of Jack's disciplinary history.
- Hattie notices Jack through unrelated federal work.
- Nina discovers the gala.
- Reese identifies the shaft and hides an ankle failure.
- Chelsea confirms the ledger and destruction plan.
- Jack withholds Chelsea's identity from the crew.
- Marcus sets an abort threshold.

### Gala night

- Crew enters in assigned roles.
- Hattie is present on unrelated work.
- Rylan tightens access.
- Reese enters the shaft.
- Jack's pressure causes the ledger movement to accelerate.
- Chelsea and two employees are sent to the archive level.
- Fire suppression below the ballroom is disabled.
- Jack realizes the witnesses are intended losses and withholds the full danger.
- Marcus declares the abort threshold crossed.
- Jack refuses abort.
- Reese's ankle fails; she falls and sustains serious leg injury.
- Anton recovers the ledger.
- Controlled fire begins.
- Marcus learns Jack knew.
- Jack confronts and disables Rylan.
- Crew extracts Chelsea, employees, Reese, and ledger evidence.
- Nina distributes authenticated pages and supporting records.

### Aftermath

- Stephen's framing is exposed.
- Avery receives the truth.
- Chelsea survives and can explain the system.
- Reese undergoes surgery.
- Hattie reopens Jack's file.
- Marcus and Jack remain fractured.
- Addison reviews the failed operation and identifies Jack as the leading explanation.
